## The rule (derivation logic, as committed)

Evidence layers, strongest first; every verdict names the layer that decided it.
Pattern alone can NEVER return single — single requires inspected source structure.

- L0 adapter shape: SWORD / helloao-style one-work-by-construction adapters → single.
- L2 source ThML `DC.Creator` roles: ≥2 distinct declared-author clusters → composite
  (L2a); declared author absent from the source's authors while present as
  editor/translator → composite (L2b).
- L3 the source's own table of contents (ThML div1/div2 titles from the local CCEL
  cache or a live fetch of `https://www.ccel.org/ccel/<id>.xml`; Gutenberg ebook
  contents): >1 distinct top-level work → composite; a numbered run of genre-titled
  treatises → composite (L3b). Numbered parts, per-biblical-book commentary parts,
  part-families ("A Treatise of X / of Y"), parallel-language editions, and halves of
  one whole count as parts, not works. Hygiene-class divisions (title pages,
  contents, indexes, prefaces, editorial matter) are filtered before counting.
- L4 a non-chapter division title attributing authorship/biographical subject to a
  named person other than the declared author ("Life of Dr. X", "Letter of Ser Y",
  "Sermon of Dr. Z") → composite; "Life of <the declared author>" counts (a biography
  of the author is another hand by construction). Scripture attributions ("the
  Epistle of Paul to the Romans") are a closed class and do not fire.
- L5 the manifest entry's own shape: title attributes the work to a named person ≠
  author; "Works of X, Vol. N" collection shape; "X and Y <surname>" two-author
  title; author field declaring more than one person → composite.
- Dev DB (dev-staged works, `--dev-db`, `NEON_BRANCH=dev`, SELECT only): staged
  section headings scanned with the same L4 markers as supplementary evidence.
  NOTE, measured before registration: the staged `sections` headings are a lossy
  flattening — schaff-anf06 stages 563 sections headed only "Chapter I…" with all
  div1 author boundaries (Gregory Thaumaturgus, Dionysius, Julius Africanus, …) lost —
  so the CCEL source TOC, not the staged headings, is the authoritative structure;
  the DB is a cross-check, not the primary source.

Red-proof (THE_LOOP rule 4), from pre-registration QA: the rule flags the 24-slug
structural-hold list members it can see (anselm-basic-works, jfb, the schaff volumes,
donne-devotions, flavel-life, tolstoy-maupassant) as composite while returning all 38
dev58-pass works single — the check goes both red and green on non-key works.

disclosed: strict-TypeScript non-null annotations to make the repo's

Why "composite" inside the 314 does not rescue the batch's property, stated plainly:
the 190 splits into two populations the rule cannot separate without the open-class
title inference this order exists to forbid —

- **Apparent TRUE composites the 24-slug structural-hold list missed**, i.e. works the
  two-axis partition passed that are not one-work-one-author by the source's own
  structure. The hardest evidence: `cowper-guyonpoems` (DC.Creator declares Cowper AND
  guyon), `fenelon-progress` (DC.Creator declares Fenelon AND Guyon), `mcgarvey-gospels`
  (DC.Creator declares McGarvey AND Pendelton), `kierkegaard-selections` (TOC:
  Diapsalmata / The Banquet / Fear and Trembling / … — a selections anthology),
  `law-humbleearnest` (Address to the Clergy + Collection of Letters + Letters to a
  Lady), `boethius-tracts` (five distinct theological tracts), `gardner-cell` (seven
  early mystical treatises), `drummond-greatest` / `drummond-ideal` / `drummond-life`
  (booklet collections, two with memorial sketches by other hands), `kelly-gerhardtsong`
  (biographical sketch + songs), `wesley-journal` (editor apparatus + biographical
  sketch + journal), `ruysbroeck-adornment` (two treatises), `teresa-life` (annals by
  another hand + the Life), `barclay-quakers` (account of Barclay's life and writings +
  the Apology). If even a handful of these are what their TOCs say they are, the
  "detector-PASS AND structurally clean" partition under-held, and the flippable set is
  not established as 1:1.
- **Rule false positives on single works whose chapters carry thematic titles or
  unnumbered front/back matter** — e.g. `murray-true-vine` ("The Vine", "The
  Husbandman" — chapters), `boethius-consolation` (Books I–V plus apparatus),
  `defoe-crusoe` / `macdonald-sirgibbie` (novels in numbered chapters plus a "Note" or
  "FOOTNOTES" division), `more-comfort` ("Dialogue of Comfort" — not a person),
  `boettner-predest` / `pink-just` ("Doctrine of Predestination/Justification" — not a
  person), `bernard-st-malachy` (a Life of Malachy written BY Bernard — biography of a
  subject is the author's own work), `augustine-enchiridion` ("the life of the world to
  come" — not a person). Two outright bugs observed in the committed rule and left
  unfixed per the order: the dev-DB heading path (L4-db) applies its markers without
  the numbered-chapter and per-biblical-book skips the ThML path applies (sole cause of
  the `chesterton-aquinas` and `neander-a-expo-phil` flags), and the person-name test
  over-fires on all-capitalized non-person phrases ("Comfort Against Tribulation").

A TOC title alone cannot distinguish "thematically titled chapter of one work" from
"bound-in distinct work" — that is the same open-class wall that cost two detector
rounds, now measured from the structural side: on this rule's own showing, the
1:1 property is established for 124 of 314 works and contradicted-or-unproven for 190.
**The batch holds.** Recommended next step (not executed here — one validation job):
hand-triage the 190 with the rule's evidence lines as the reading list, and treat the
DC.Creator/L3 true-composite candidates above as the first carve-out of any revised
flippable set; the 124 single returns are the only works whose structure this rule
actually established.
