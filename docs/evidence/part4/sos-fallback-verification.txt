## VERDICT: THE CONDITION IS NOT MET. The no-content fallback does NOT fire on SoS queries.
## The user-facing outcome is nonetheless SAFE today — but by accident, not by design.

## Premise (confirmed)
  commentary_entries rows for Song of Solomon (book 22): 0
  (control: the same query shape returns rows for other books, so 0 is real)

## Stage 1 — retrieval (verify-sos-fallback.mts): 0 of 4 SoS queries fall back
  "Song of Solomon 2, I am the rose of Sharon..."          retrieval.length = 6  -> NO fallback
  "Song of Solomon 4, thou art all fair my love..."        retrieval.length = 6  -> NO fallback
  "What do the commentators say about the Song of Songs?"  retrieval.length = 6  -> NO fallback
  "Canticles 8, love is strong as death..."                retrieval.length = 6  -> NO fallback
  CONTROL "John 10, I am the good shepherd"                retrieval.length = 6  (pipeline alive)

  WHAT IT RETURNS INSTEAD — sources that CANNOT contain Song of Solomon commentary:
    - Barnes' Notes on the NEW TESTAMENT              (SoS is Old Testament)
    - Wesley's Explanatory Notes on the NEW TESTAMENT (same)
    - Homilies of John Chrysostom (Matthew, John, Acts, Romans, Corinthians, Hebrews, Paulines)
    - Augustine, Exposition on PSALM 45
  Scores as low as 0.005 / 0.007 / 0.012 are still returned: retrieveCommentary takes top-K with
  NO RELEVANCE FLOOR, so an empty-coverage book yields six confident-looking irrelevant chunks
  rather than nothing. That is the mechanism, and it is structural, not query-specific.

## Stage 2 — full teach() loop (verify-sos-endtoend.mts): user outcome is `kind:'fallback'`
  "Song of Solomon 2..."  -> kind = fallback; verifier violations: a wall of SCHEMA errors
                             (/blocks/N must have required property 'text', type must equal
                             constant, anchors start/end must be integer, ...)
  "Canticles 8..."        -> kind = fallback; verifier violations:
                             anchor_valid   "anchor 46080604-46080604 is not a valid canonical verse range"
                             passage_valid  "passage 46080604-46080604 is not a valid canonical verse range"
  Both: retrieval kept = 6 sources; the user sees RAW SOURCES, not composed prose.

## Why this matters (the honest reading)
1. NO fabricated Song of Solomon exposition reached a user. Fail-closed held. Good.
2. But the guard that held was the VERIFIER, catching INCIDENTAL downstream symptoms — malformed
   schema, and an anchor (46080604 = book 46, 1 Corinthians) that is not a valid verse range. The
   system never detected "we have no Song of Solomon sources". `kind:'empty'` never fired.
3. So the safety is not attributable to coverage detection. A well-formed, schema-valid answer
   with valid anchors, grounded in the retrieved NON-SoS sources, has no obvious reason to be
   rejected — and would be served as a composed answer about Song of Solomon built out of New
   Testament commentary. Nothing measured here rules that out; the two runs simply did not
   produce one.
4. Even on the safe path, the user asking about Song of Solomon is shown six sources that are not
   about Song of Solomon, presented as what others have said. That is a relevance failure at
   minimum.

## Consequence for the ruling
"ACCEPTED LIMITATION (coverage hole, not a quality failure)" does not survive contact with this
evidence as stated, because the condition attached to it is not met. The gap is not that SoS
returns nothing; it is that SoS returns the wrong thing and is caught downstream for unrelated
reasons. ESCALATED back to the owner rather than written up as ruled.

## Sample size
n=4 SoS queries. Small — but 4/4 consistent, and the mechanism (no relevance floor on a
zero-coverage book) is structural rather than query-specific, so the mechanism claim is robust
even though the rate is not precisely estimated.
