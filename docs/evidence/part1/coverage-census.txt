## MY TWO ERRORS, both reaching a confident conclusion from the wrong table

  ERROR 1 (earlier, when the SoS finding was first reported).
  I queried `commentary_entries WHERE verse_start >= 22000000 AND verse_start < 23000000` -> 0 rows,
  and reported "commentary_entries has 0 Song of Solomon rows (control: the query shape works)".
  BOTH halves were wrong:
    * `commentary_entries.verse_start` is a VERSE NUMBER WITHIN A CHAPTER (observed range 1..71),

  I got the right answer (0) for entirely the wrong reason — a false negative that happened to
  coincide with the truth. THE_LOOP rule 1, exactly: name the artifact before you trust the number.

## Why this matters for row 3b's prescription

  The falsified fix was a RELEVANCE FLOOR. The scores cannot separate on-label from off-label
  (best on-label 0.9957 vs worst off-label 0.9999), and `score` is two different quantities —
  reranker for leads, vector cosine (0.55-0.75) for backfilled second voices — so any floor >=0.9
  deletes every backfilled second voice and breaks the >=2-voices guarantee on HEALTHY queries.
  The scores are right about TEXT and wrong about PASSAGE.

  The corrected fix is ON-PASSAGE COVERAGE DETECTION: fire kind:'empty' when zero returned chunks
  have a verseId intersecting the asked range. For a SoS verse-ref query that is exactly right —
  there are genuinely 0 legal rows for that book, so "no sources" is the honest answer, not a mask
  over a ranking bug. (Had error 2 been true, coverage detection WOULD have been masking a ranking
  bug; it is worth recording that the two diagnoses imply different fixes, which is why the artifact
  mattered so much here.)

  Topical queries have no asked range and are coverage-blind by construction — not solved by this,
  and stated as such in row 3b rather than implied away.
